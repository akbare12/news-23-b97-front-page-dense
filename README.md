Sum up 23 November's world news in one image — 31 stories: the AI rout that hit Asia
(Kospi −3.3%), Guterres urging AI rules in Bridgetown, and the US lead over China at a
record low in the Asia Power Index 2026, plus 10 more stories, 18 also-on-Monday items,
a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps 22/21 Nov stories out.

Run `python3 main.py` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; it copies the validated
1024×1024 `entry.png` (stdlib only, no PIL) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
`draw.py` (PIL) regenerates the image from scratch.

Sources: Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, AFP, Yonhap, BBC, WSJ, ESPN, Lowy Institute.
